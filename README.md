## 🚨 The Real-World Problem
As computer engineering students, the pressure to balance heavy university assignments, daily DSA problem-solving, and building full-stack projects with AI integrations creates intense decision fatigue. The result? We spend more time worrying about *what* to study than actually executing it. Burnout and endless scrolling take over. 

Most productivity tools fail because they mix planning with execution, adding to the overwhelming cognitive load.

## 💡 Our Solution: The Two-Step Focus Framework
**Next Hour** physically separates the planning phase from the execution phase to eliminate distractions and force deep work.

### Phase 1: The Brain (AWS AI Engine)
We leveraged **AWS PartyRock** to build a strict AI coaching brain. Instead of a generic chat, you input your subject and available time constraints. The AI cuts through the noise and outputs **one single micro-task** and a personalized kawaii spirit companion. Planning is done. 

👉 **[Enter the AI Session Here]** *https://partyrock.aws/u/insanhii/kFYmDS61S/NEXT-HOUR*

### Phase 2: The Muscle (Execution Dashboard)
Once the task is locked, users leave the AI behind and enter our custom **React Execution Dashboard**. No generative AI text, no scrolling—just a sleek, minimalist dark-mode countdown timer. When the deep-work block hits zero, a visual confetti explosion and dynamic success messages trigger a dopamine loop of getting things done.

👉 **[Live Vercel Deployment]**(https://next-hour-nine.vercel.app/)
