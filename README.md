## 🚨 The Real-World Problem

As computer engineering students, the pressure to balance heavy university assignments, daily DSA problem-solving, and building full-stack projects creates intense decision fatigue. The result? We spend more time worrying about *what* to study than actually doing it. Burnout and endless scrolling take over.

Most productivity tools fail because they mix planning with execution, and that adds more load.

## 💡 Our Solution: The Two-Step Focus Framework

**Next Hour** separates planning from execution.

### 🧠 Phase 1: The Brain (AWS AI Engine)

We used **AWS PartyRock** as a strict study coach.  
You type your subject, pending work, and time.  
The AI returns **one micro-task**, a skip list, and a stop time. Planning is done.

👉 [Open the PartyRock session](https://partyrock.aws/u/insanhii/kFYmDS61S/NEXT-HOUR)

### 💪 Phase 2: The Muscle (Execution Dashboard)

Then you leave the AI and open the React timer.  
No extra chat. No scrolling. Only a countdown.  
When the block hits zero: confetti + a success screen.

👉 [Open the live dashboard](https://next-hour-nine.vercel.app/)
